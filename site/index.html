How it works

Small experiments, before the big spend.

  1. 01

    Seal

    The vendor uploads the environment to a sealed sandbox. You never see the tasks, only the results.

  2. 02

    Train small

    We train small open models on it at four sizes with one fixed recipe. Cheap, fast and repeatable.

  3. 03

    Measure

    We test the models on private problems they never trained on, and try to fool the grader.

  4. 04

    Forecast and price

    We predict the gain at your model size and put a fair price on each task.
